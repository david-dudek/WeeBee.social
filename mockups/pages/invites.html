Inferred, not specified. SPEC §4.2 gives the mechanics of the invite budget but no section of either document describes a screen for seeing it or sending an invite — this mockup builds the minimum those mechanics imply. Treat this page as closer to a proposal than a mockup of something already designed.

The only way to join WeeBee is by invitation, so when you'd like a friend or family member to join, you can send them one here — a single-use link they'll use to create their account.

SPEC §4.1: "an invite is a single-use link/code sent by email."

You have 3 invites saved up right now, out of a possible 5. You'll get one more in 12 days.

SPEC §17 bans visible counts generally ("no likes or visible counts... none exist"), but an invite budget nobody can see is one nobody can use — arguably the one number the platform has to show. It's written here as an ordinary sentence rather than as a progress bar or a badge, per this session's own instructions — see mockups/NOTES.md. INVITE_BANK_MAX = 5, INVITE_REPLENISH_DAYS = 30, new accounts start with INVITE_NEW_ACCOUNT = 2 (§4.2).

If you don't have any invites saved up, this won't do anything until your next one arrives — you get one every 30 days, up to 5 saved at a time.
