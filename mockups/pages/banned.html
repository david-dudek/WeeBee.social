Your account

Your account has been suspended by the person who runs WeeBee. While it's suspended, nothing you've posted or shared is visible to anyone else here.

SPEC §13.2.1: "states plainly that the account has been suspended by the operator and that their content is hidden from other members." No reason is given — "reasons are the deferred policy question, and a generated reason line would promise a consistency the v1 workflow cannot keep" — so none is written here.

Harness limitation, not a design choice. SPEC §13.2.1 requires this page to carry no navigation at all — "everything else... is that page" — and the M7 prompt repeats it explicitly: "this means the page does not use the standard _nav.html." This mockup set wraps every page in the same shared partials/_base.html, header and _nav.html included, with no per-page way to omit it; doing that would mean editing partials/, outside this session's touched files (mockups/pages/, CRIB.md, NOTES.md, pages/index.html). The nav links visible above this page's own content are therefore an artifact of the review harness, not something the real, live page would ever show — see mockups/NOTES.md. Nothing inside the content below links anywhere except data export and account deletion, which is the part this mockup can actually be faithful to.

What you can still do

You can download a complete copy of everything you've put into WeeBee, or delete your account for good. Everything else here — your feed, posting, commenting, friend requests, discovering people, reporting, and the rest of your settings — doesn't work while your account is suspended.
