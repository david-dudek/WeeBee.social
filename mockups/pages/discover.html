SPEC §11.4: "One dedicated page the user must deliberately visit." Pull-only — nothing here is pushed anywhere else. No suggestion, match or visit is ever mentioned in the feed, nothing is ever announced to anyone, and there is no "someone viewed you" in either direction (§11.4).

People you may know

Friends-of-friends only — never a stranger three hops away, which is a permanent ceiling, not a setting (§11.1). Each is ranked by shared mutual friends and shared profile hashtags and shown with auto-context, exactly in the form SPEC's own example gives: "knows Alice and Tom · shares #hiking."

No rank number, match score, or count is shown for either suggestion — none exists anywhere on this page (§17). Jordan's context line carries no "shares #x" clause at all, because Jordan and David share no hashtag: the auto-context states only what is actually true, never a placeholder for a match that isn't there.

Matched posts

Hashtag-gated profile posts from friends-of-friends who share a tag with David (§11.3) — the same gate that governs what Priya sees on David's own profile, running in the other direction.

    Visible to: all of Priya's friends, and friends-of-friends with any of #hiking.

Nothing above is a count, a rank, or a "you matched!" moment. Nothing on this page suggests a push, a notification, or that anyone has been told David visited (§11.4). There is also no way to browse #jazz, #cornwall or #baking globally from here, or from anywhere else on the platform — the only tag view that exists is the viewer-scoped filter below, reached by clicking a tag, never a standing "all posts" list (§11.2, §17).
