Preview — as a friend

SPEC §9.5, mode 1: the owner can preview their own profile and contact card exactly as one specific friend would see them. This mode exists because both surfaces are per-friend — the contact card follows the per-friend visibility cascade of §10.3, and the mutual-friend block on the profile itself depends on who is looking — so only naming a friend makes either concrete.
