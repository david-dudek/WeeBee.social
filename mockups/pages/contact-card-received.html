Requesting a contact card

SPEC §10.4: a friend requests another friend's contact card via a picker — no text — and the system auto-replies with exactly the version of the card that requester is permitted to see, resolved through the full visibility cascade of §10.3. This page renders what two different requesters get back from David's card, and the request-more-access flags §10.5 adds on top.

What Alice receives

Alice taps "Request contact card" on David's profile — no text field, nothing to type — and the system replies immediately with this:

  • Phone

    07700 900123

  • Personal email

    david@example.invalid

  • WhatsApp

    wa dot me slash [David's WhatsApp link]

The same three items, resolved the same way, as M4's preview-as-friend.html already showed for David previewing Alice's view — Signal is absent because David has an individual override turning it off for her specifically (see the editor). This is that same auto-reply, framed here as what Alice's own request actually returns rather than David's preview of it.

The empty case

Henry requests David's card the same way. David has individually turned off both Phone and Signal for Henry — the only two items that default on for friends generally — so nothing else on the card applies to him either. Henry gets this back:

You haven't shared anything on your contact card with Henry.

Not an error — a real, honest empty state, exactly as the prompt for this session asks it to be treated. The specific overrides that produce it are a constructed worked example, the same kind of deliberate fact this track has invented before purely to demonstrate a mechanism (see NOTES.md on Jordan's characterization) — they say nothing about David and Henry's actual relationship.

Request more access (may ship in v1.1)

§10.5: if the card that comes back is insufficient, the requester can ask for more, without composing any text.

What Henry can do

Ask David for more

Henry can turn any of these off again at any time.

What David sees

A one-time notification when Henry first sets a flag, then a small passive flag next to Henry's name wherever David encounters it — his profile, his comments — for as long as he keeps it on. No repeat notifications, no nagging.

  • Henry

    Asked for more contact info: email

Muting hides the flag from David's feed and comments; it stays visible on Henry's own profile page, to David only, for as long as Henry keeps it on.

This whole section carries SPEC's own hedge, "may ship in v1.1" (§10.5), and is labelled with it here rather than presented as settled v1 — everything above it on this page is the v1 card-request flow itself.
