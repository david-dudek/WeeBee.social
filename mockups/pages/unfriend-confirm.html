Unfriend or block Sofia?

Unfriend Sofia

Unfriending is silent — Sofia receives no notification, and the effect is immediate: she loses access to your friends-only content, your feed stops receiving hers, and each of you disappears from the other's audience lists.

Unfriending is not invisibility. Sofia doesn't vanish; she falls to whatever tier she still qualifies for. §5.3, verbatim:

They'll no longer see your posts or your about section. If you have friends in common, they can still see your name, photo and short bio — and any blog post tagged with an interest you both share.

Her own past comments on your posts stay where they are; only account deletion removes those (§4.7).

Want the stronger outcome? Block instead

"A user who wants the stronger outcome wants a block" — §5.3's own framing. Blocking is silent and fully mutually invisible (§5.4). When you block Sofia, or she blocks you, order doesn't matter:

  • Any friendship between you ends, silently.
  • Neither of you appears in the other's discovery, suggestions, hashtag matches, mutual-friend lists, or introduction flows.
  • Neither of you can see the other's comments or reactions anywhere, including on shared friends' posts — others still see them; the invisibility is only between the two of you.
  • Neither of you can send the other a friend request, an introduction, or a contact-card request.
  • Neither of you is shown the other's existence in any list or count.

Block lists are private. Only the person who placed the block can undo it.

Sofia is one of the five plain friend names CRIB.md §3 adds "purely to give friends.html's alphabetical list something to sort" — chosen here only because she carries no prior storyline this action would sit oddly against. Block's mutual invisibility can't be demonstrated as two side-by-side viewer states on a single static page, the same limitation NOTES.md's entry on profile-fof-tagged.html already records for the same underlying rule — this page states the effects in text rather than attempting to fake a second viewer's session to show them.
