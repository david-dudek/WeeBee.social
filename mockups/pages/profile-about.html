Extended bio

Spent most of this year rediscovering things I used to be impatient about: bread that needs a day to prove, a garden that ignores my schedule entirely, a trail up the ridge that hasn't gotten any shorter since I was twenty. None of it is efficient and I've stopped apologizing for that.

We keep saying we'll finally walk the whole coast path together instead of just the one weekend a year we manage — here's the stretch I'm angling for next spring, no excuses this time. Always happy to talk baking disasters, terrible garden puns, or where to find decent coffee near the ridge trailhead.
