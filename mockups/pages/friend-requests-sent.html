The send hold, after a profile change

§5.2 and §13.6: sending is blocked for REQUEST_HOLD_AFTER_PROFILE_CHANGE_HOURS = 12 hours after either your profile photo or your short bio changes, so the card already delivered to Jordan above can't be rewritten by a later edit, and the surface can't be used as a rotating billboard between batches of requests. Changing the photo or bio itself stays free and unlimited (§13.6) — the hold sits on the push, not the edit.

You can send friend requests again in 9 hours. You changed your profile photo 3 hours ago, and WeeBee holds new requests for 12 hours after a photo or short bio change.

Verbatim per §5.2: "you can send friend requests again in N hours," stating the reason rather than just the number. Four carve-outs trigger no hold at all (§13.6): picking an image from the platform's default avatar set, clearing the short bio to empty, a save rejected by screening, and any change to the extended bio — none of them shown here, since this page renders the one blocking case the prompt asks for.

The friend cap

§5.1: FRIEND_CAP = 300. Any action that would push either party past it fails with a clear, honest error message — never silently. It can be your own cap or theirs:

This can't be sent: it would put you over the 300-friend limit. Nothing is sent until you're below the cap — unfriending someone, or waiting for an old request to expire, brings you back under it.

This can't be sent: the person you're trying to reach already has 300 friends, the most WeeBee allows. Nothing is sent, and they are never notified that you tried.

No named recipient is used for either message — unlike Jordan's pending request above, the friend cap isn't tied to any specific person in this track's small cast, so the wording stays generic rather than inventing a character solely to be "at the cap."
