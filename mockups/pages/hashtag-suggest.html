Suggest a hashtag

Every interest tag on WeeBee — on a profile or in a post — comes from one shared list, picked through search rather than typed freely. This page shows what happens when your search doesn't find anything on that list, and the form it offers you instead.

SPEC §11.2: hashtags are never free-typed; every tag comes from the operator-curated HASHTAG_VOCAB. §11.2.1 is this page's own section: "when a search matches no tag and no alias, the picker says so plainly and offers the suggestion form — it never presents a text field that would accept a tag and then silently fail to create one."

Searching for an interest

No interest matches "pottery."

Some interests on our list go by more than one name behind the scenes, so searching a different word for the same idea should still find it — but "pottery" doesn't match anything we've got, under any of the words we check for it either.

SPEC §11.2.1: every vocabulary entry carries operator-curated search aliases (e.g. #hiking also matches "hikes," "rambling," "trekking," "trail walking") that are never displayed, never selectable, and are not tags themselves — they exist only so a search under a different word for the same interest still finds the right entry. This empty result means "pottery" matched none of the vocabulary's tags or their aliases.

Suggest it instead

If you think "pottery" should be an interest people can tag themselves and their posts with, you can suggest it here. Suggestions are read in batches by the person who runs WeeBee — there's no reply, and no way to check back on this one. The most common outcome isn't a brand new tag at all: usually it turns out an existing interest already covers it, and your word just gets added as another way to find it, which fixes the search for everyone from then on.

SPEC §13.5, §11.2.1, v1.21: the confirmation copy does the whole job here, since no reply is ever sent — "submissions are read in batches, no reply is sent, and an accepted change simply appears where it belongs." This page reuses the same category dropdown as the general operator-request form, since a hashtag suggestion feeds the identical queue, just pre-selected and pre-filled with the search that came up empty.
