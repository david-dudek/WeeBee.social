SPEC §12 opening: notification delivery is "in-feed (§7.7), plus optional email" — this is that optional copy, sent only because settings.html's "Also send some notifications to my email" is turned on. §12.2 governs the wording exactly as it does in-feed: the actor's names, the event type in specific plain text, "Alice and Tom commented on your post" reused verbatim from mockups/CRIB.md §2 — never an excerpt of what they actually wrote, and names rather than a count ("3 new comments" is explicitly banned).

No link, unlike the in-feed version of this same notification already built on feed.html (M1). §12.2 itself says a notification "carries... a link," and this session's own item 9 repeats that. But this session's own rule 1 and its own before-you-finish checklist are both unqualified: "invitations remain links, and only invitations," and "no link in any email except invite" — and invite.html's own body tells the reader it is "the only email WeeBee ever sends with a link inside it," which a link here would make false. Read together, the more specific and more emphatic rule for the email channel wins over §12.2's more general description of a notification, which was written primarily with the in-feed rendering in mind. The emailed copy therefore points the reader back to the app in plain text instead of a clickable link; the in-feed notification itself is unaffected and keeps its link. Flagged here as a real tension between two SPEC-adjacent instructions, not silently resolved — see mockups/NOTES.md.

No age of any kind, not even an absolute one. §12.3 (rule 2): email carries no relative age, and this template isn't one of the two narrow exceptions (security events, deletion warnings) that get an absolute one either — so this notification states no time reference at all. §12.3's own reasoning: "the mail client's own received-time is more accurate than anything the body could assert." Noted per this session's own explicit instruction to record this in mockups/NOTES.md.
