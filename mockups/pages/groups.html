Groups

Inferred, not specified. SPEC §6 gives the rules groups must follow but never describes a screen for managing them — this mockup builds the minimum screen those rules imply. Treat this page as closer to a proposal than a mockup of something already designed.

Groups are a private way to organize your own friends, so you can pick them quickly when you're choosing who sees a post or setting up your contact card. Nobody in a group knows they've been put in one — a group is just for you.

SPEC §6: "Groups are private to their owner; members never know they are in one." A friend can be in zero, one, or several of a user's own groups.

Create a group

Who's in this group?

WeeBee never creates, fills in, or changes a group on its own — every friend in every group above got there because you put them there. The one exception isn't really an exception: after you've hand-picked the same people for a post a few times, the composer may offer to save that exact selection as a group for you to name, which is only a memory of choices you already made, never a guess about how close you are to anyone.

SPEC §6: "the platform must not auto-create, auto-populate, or auto-modify groups." The one permitted suggestion is the composer's "Save this selection as a group?", already shown in composer.html (section 3) and quoted verbatim in mockups/CRIB.md — "memory of the user's explicit choices, never behavioral inference about relationship strength."
