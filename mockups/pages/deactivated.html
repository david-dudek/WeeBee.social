Your account

Your account will be permanently erased on 28 September. Until then, you can change your mind by logging back in and cancelling — right here.

SPEC §4.7: the deactivation banner carries the absolute erasure date — "an account event, and therefore one of §7.5.1's two narrow exceptions to relative time." DELETE_GRACE_DAYS = 30. This is the only absolute date anywhere in this mockup set outside the security and inactivity emails, which are M8's scope and not built this session — see mockups/NOTES.md.

Nobody else on WeeBee can see you right now — your profile, your posts, your comments, all of it is hidden, the same as if you'd already deleted everything. It's reversible until the date above.

SPEC §4.7: "the account and all of its content become invisible everywhere, reversibly... nobody is notified." A visitor gets §9.3's single response, the same one errors.html demonstrates.

What happens if you don't cancel

When the 30 days are up, you'll be erased from WeeBee at once — your posts, comments, photos, friend list, everything. A copy may still exist in our encrypted backups for up to 30 days after that, until it's gone from there too.

SPEC §4.7's own wording, not softened: "erased from the platform at once, and gone from the last encrypted backup within BACKUP_RETENTION_DAYS = 30 days after that." §4.7 and §7.5 both explicitly refuse a combined "gone by day 120" phrasing, so this page states the two facts separately rather than adding them together.

Change your mind?

If you'd like to stay, you can cancel this right now and pick up exactly where you left off.

SPEC §4.7: "the user can log in to cancel." Unlike banned.html, this page keeps the ordinary shared navigation — SPEC says plainly that "the user's own view keeps working so they can cancel," a materially less restricted state than a ban's "authorization is empty" (§13.2.1).
