Your contact card

This card is how friends reach you elsewhere — phone, email, a messenger — not a rebuilt inbox. Your login email is never included unless you deliberately add it as its own item below; it's a credential, not a card entry.

SPEC §10.1: WeeBee has no free-text direct messaging, which is why this handoff card exists. The login email is governed separately, at §4.6.

The card starts empty

Until you add something, there's nothing here to share. A friend who requests an empty card gets exactly that back, not an error.

See the empty case, as a requester receives it.

Add an item

Up to 12 items total, drawn from phone numbers, email addresses, and messenger links. A messenger link is only accepted from the official domain of a recognized messenger — WhatsApp, Signal, Telegram and the like — never a bare URL.

Your items

Each item carries its own visibility, set through a three-level cascade: a default for all friends, then any group override, then an individual override, which always wins in either direction. Where a friend belongs to two groups with conflicting settings for the same item, the more restrictive one wins — deny beats allow. Accidental under-sharing is recoverable (see request flags); accidental over-sharing is not.
