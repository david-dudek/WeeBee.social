Export your data

Download a complete copy of everything you've put into WeeBee — your profile, every post and comment you've written, your friend list, your groups, your contact card, and your photos — as plain files you can open anywhere: JSON files, plus your actual images.

SPEC §4.9: profile, posts, comments authored, friend list, groups, contact card and images, "in an open format (JSON + image files)." Serves three purposes: GDPR compliance, a credible exit, and any future migration.

About the exact dates and times

Nowhere on WeeBee itself shows an exact date or time — only phrases like "a few days ago." Your export is the one place exact timestamps live. If you ever need to know precisely when something happened, this is where to find it.

SPEC §7.5.1: "the exact underlying time is deliberately absent from the interface... anyone needing exact times gets them from their data export (§4.9)." This page is what makes that promise concrete rather than hypothetical.

This page works the same way whether your account is active, in its 30-day deletion grace period, or suspended by the operator — export is a data right, not a privilege.

Linked from settings.html, banned.html and deactivated.html alike (SPEC §4.9, §15.1, §13.2.1).
