Your contact card

SPEC §10.1: WeeBee has no free-text direct messaging. This card is the handoff to the channels friends already trust — phone, email, a messenger — not a rebuilt inbox. Your login email (§4.6) is never on this card unless you deliberately add it as its own item below; it's a credential, not a card entry.

The card starts empty

Until you add something, there is nothing here to share. A friend who requests an empty card gets exactly that back — see the empty case, as a requester receives it — not an error.

Add an item

Up to CONTACT_ITEMS_MAX = 12 items total, drawn from phone numbers, email addresses, and messenger links. A messenger link is only accepted from the allowlisted official domain of a recognized messenger — WhatsApp, Signal, Telegram and the like — never a bare URL (§10.2).

What kind of item?

4 of 12 items used.

Your items

Each item carries its own visibility, set through a three-level cascade: a default for all friends, then any group override, then an individual override, which always wins in either direction. Where a friend belongs to two groups with conflicting settings for the same item, the more restrictive one wins — deny beats allow. Accidental under-sharing is recoverable (see request flags, below on the received card); accidental over-sharing is not. (§10.3)
