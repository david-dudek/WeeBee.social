It's been a while

SPEC §4.8: warning emails at 180, 365, 670 and 700 days since last login — "the first two are gentle 'your account is dormant' notes." The two shown below are those first two. Neither states how long it's been: this session's rule 2 bans a relative age in any email, full stop, and SPEC gives these two notices no absolute date to state either (that's reserved for the two deletion warnings on inactivity-deletion.html), so there is deliberately no time reference of any kind here — the wording is identical both times, since nothing in either document distinguishes their content, only their timing.
